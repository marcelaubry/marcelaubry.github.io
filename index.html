About

Hi, I'm Marcel.

I'm a Forward Deployed Engineer at Blitzy in Cambridge, MA, where I was one of the first FDEs. I take ambiguous enterprise requirements and turn them into production software, using AI-driven development to move a lot faster than teams are used to.

Before that I shipped customer-facing features at GEICO and built healthcare data pipelines. I studied Computer Engineering at Boston University: a B.S. cum laude, then an M.S. in Electrical & Computer Engineering with a 3.95 GPA.

I grew up in Toronto and speak English and French. At BU I captained the Division I track & cross-country team. I'm a 4:02 miler and still hold the Canadian U18 1000m record.

I'm particularly drawn to startups due to the ownership that comes with it. I've always been passionate about entrepreneurship and completed a Product Innovation concentration during my undergraduate studies.
